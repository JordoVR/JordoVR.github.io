Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how JordoBot handles your information when you use its account-linking features on the Jordo's Junction Discord server ("the associated server").

JordoBot allows you to link your Discord account to your VRChat account. This connection helps organize VRChat display names according to Patreon-related roles in the associated Discord server, supporting functionality for the associated VRChat worlds.

This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, where it is made available, and how you can request its deletion.

2. Information We Collect

When you link your accounts, JordoBot collects and stores the following information:

  • Discord User ID: Used to identify your Discord account and associate it with your VRChat account.
  • VRChat User ID: Used to identify your VRChat account and allow you to update your stored display name if it changes.
  • VRChat Display Name: Used in the public JSON lists that support the associated VRChat worlds.

Your Discord User ID and VRChat User ID are stored together in an external database. Your VRChat display name is used to populate the appropriate lists in the public JSON file according to your Patreon-related roles.

JordoBot's account-linking database does not intentionally store your Discord messages, email address, or other Discord profile information.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Link your Discord account to your VRChat account.
  • Keep your VRChat display name up to date, including when you use the "Update Your Name" feature.
  • Include your VRChat display name in the appropriate public lists based on your Patreon-related roles in the associated Discord server.
  • Identify linked accounts so the appropriate Discord role can be assigned.
  • Delete your stored account-linking information when you unlink your account or leave the associated Discord server.

Your information is used to provide and maintain these features. JordoBot does not sell your personal information.

4. Public VRChat Display Name Lists

JordoBot maintains a publicly accessible JSON file containing VRChat display names organized into lists according to Patreon-related roles.

Public JSON file

View patreon.json

If you link your account, your VRChat display name may appear in one or more lists in this file, depending on your roles in the associated Discord server.

The public JSON file contains VRChat display names rather than the association between your Discord User ID and VRChat User ID stored in the external database.

Because the file is public, other people may view, download, or copy its contents. Removing your display name from the original file cannot guarantee that previously downloaded or cached copies will also be removed.

5. Data Storage and Security

The association between your Discord User ID and VRChat User ID is stored in an external database. The public VRChat display name lists are hosted separately through GitHub Pages.

Reasonable measures have been taken to protect stored information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

JordoBot is designed to retain your account-linking information only for as long as it is needed to provide its features.

Unlinking your account

You can use the "Unlink Your Account" button in the #link-discord-vrchat channel on the associated Discord server to have JordoBot initiate deletion of your stored account-linking information from the external database.

Leaving the Discord server

If you leave the associated Discord server, JordoBot will initiate deletion of your stored account-linking information from the external database.

Deletion from the external database and removal from the public JSON file are separate operations. Your VRChat display name should also be removed from the public lists as part of the associated cleanup process. These operations may occur separately, and removal from the public lists depends on the cleanup process completing successfully.

Copies of public information that other people have already downloaded may remain outside JordoBot's control.

7. Sharing Your Information

JordoBot does not sell your personal information. Your Discord User ID and VRChat User ID are stored together in the external database to provide the account-linking service.

Your VRChat display name may be visible to the public through the JSON file described in Section 4.

JordoBot relies on third-party services to operate, including Discord and GitHub Pages. These services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

8. Third-Party Services

Your use of JordoBot involves third-party platforms:

  • Discord: The platform through which you interact with JordoBot.
  • VRChat: The platform associated with the VRChat account you link.
  • Patreon: Relevant to the membership-related roles used to organize VRChat display names.
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts the public JSON file.

Each third-party service has its own terms and privacy policy. JordoBot does not control the independent privacy practices of these providers.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Linking your accounts is optional. If you no longer wish to use this feature, you can unlink your account using JordoBot's "Unlink Your Account" button.

You can also use the "Update Your Name" feature if your VRChat display name changes.

Leaving the associated Discord server also triggers the deletion process described in this policy.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

For questions or privacy requests, contact the JordoBot operator using the details in the Contact section below.

10. Children's Privacy

JordoBot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users must meet the applicable age requirements for Discord, VRChat, Patreon, and any other relevant services.

If you believe your information has been handled inappropriately, please contact the operator.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to JordoBot or its data practices.

Any revised policy will be published on this page, and the last-updated date will be changed accordingly.
